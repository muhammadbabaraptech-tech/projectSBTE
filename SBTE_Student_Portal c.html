Evaluation Policy & Rubrics

How the final monthly score is weighted across every assessed category, plus the standing and grade bands used everywhere else in this app.

Weight Distribution
How the final score is built
  • Every category is first marked out of 100, then scaled down to its listed weight and summed into one Final Weighted Monthly Score out of 100.
  • All scores and weighted contributions are rounded to one decimal place for display.
  • A student's Class Rank on their report card is their position by final score among every student in the same batch and month.
  • Vs Batch Avg compares a student's final score to the mean final score of their batch for that month.
  • Admin edits to a record overwrite the previous values directly — there is no separate revision history.
